# Fool's Chapeau
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Origins:** An infamous gnome prankster.

**Description:** Looks sophisticated on a shelf, but turns into a ridiculous, plumage-covered abomination when worn.

**Mechanics:**
* **Spells:** While wearing the hat, you can cast *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* (DC 15) as an action by expending 1 charge.
* **Charges:** The hat has **3 charges** and regains all charges daily at midnight.
* **Curse:** As soon as it is put on, you look ridiculous. You have **disadvantage on all Persuasion and Intimidation checks**. The hat requires *Remove Curse* to take off.
