# Doomsight
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** An overly generous beholder.

**Description:** A highly exquisite monocle made of gold and silver. It grants some of the power that beholders are capable of invoking.

**Mechanics:**
* **Darkvision:** You gain Darkvision out to 120ft.
* **Eye Rays:** The monocle has **4 charges** and regains 1d4 charges daily at dawn. As a bonus action, you can expend a charge to focus on a target, unleashing a random ray (Roll d4):
    1.  **Fear Ray:** DC 13 Wisdom save or be frightened for 1 minute (repeat save at end of turns).
    2.  **Death Ray:** DC 13 Constitution save or take **3d10 necrotic damage** (half on success).
    3.  **Force Ray:** DC 15 Strength save or be pushed 30ft (half distance on success).
    4.  **Illusion Ray:** DC 13 Intelligence save or believe they are staring at a brick wall in all directions (repeat save at end of turns).
