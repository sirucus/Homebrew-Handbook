a sub race of those who "Gestated" and grew up near [[the flawless expanse]].

they are unable to use magic but it has left them more resistant to magics effects. to over come this limitation they have taken to using their soul to empower them.

they find being away from the wild magic of [[the flawless expanse]] to be uncomfortable, sometimes even painful, depending on how powerful they are. [[The Flawed King, Eldric Stormblade]] shouldnt be able to leave at all, but no one has every seen him seem to suffer from it.

they almost always have some feature that depict the way the wild magic has affected them

they have advantage on saving throws to resist the wild magic of [[the flawless expanse]]

they have ages like elves

### Spellsick

#### Magical Resillience

The wild magic churning in your body and soul enhances your abiltity to defend yourself against the magic of others. You have advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws against magic, and once per day you can reroll any failed saving throw against magic.

#### Weave tear

You can use your soul's binding to the weave to tear open reality and travel a short distance. As a bonus action you can teleport 30 feet into any unoccupied space.

#### Innate Spellcasting

You learn the Thaumaturgy cantrip.  
You can cast Chaos Bolt (At first level or highest available spellslot available to your, up to level 5) once between long rest without using a spell slot.  
At 3rd level you can cast Magic Weapon once between long rests without using a spell slot.  
Wisdom is your spellcasting modifier for these spells. 

#### Antimagic Weakness

Your body and soul are dependant on the magic flowing freely in the world and when this dependancy is severed you feel the effects as severe internal pain. You are vulnarable to spells and effects that cancel or dispell magic. You take 3d6 psychic damage if you are the target of either a Counterspell or Dispel magic spell. This damage is negated if the Counterspell or Dispel Magic fails. You take 3d8 psychic everytime you start your turn inside of an antimagic field.  
If you are targeted by a successful Counterspell or Dispel Magic spell you lose any ability granted by your Spellscar for 1 minute.

#### Spellscar

The Spellsickness affects many differently with different effects, The cancerlike corruption of the Spellplague has several common starting locations; The extremeties such as feet or hands, The eyes or the heart. Someone with their legs and arms spellscarred with find it easier to run and climb but live with the constant aching of their affected limb and the sight of their arms and legs being partially phased out into the ethereal plane. Those with their one of their eyes scarred have improved eyesight but their brightly glowing eye and their face deformed by bulging veins of magic. The rest have a brightly glowing heart in the middle of their chest and lightinglike glowing scars spreading across their body. The more a Spellsick is exposed to wild magics or embracing the corruption will cause the Spellscar to grow and spread further.

#### Languages

You know how to speak, read and write Common and one language of your choice.

### Eye of Mystra

The corruption of the spellsickness has consumed your eye and turned it into an orb of whirling magic. you still have the ability to see through it but are unsure how. If you focus hard enough you can see the energies of the world flowing and attain improved eyesight in the dark. Overtime the orb will grow tendrils that will extend from the sides of your eye and burrow into your head, neck and shoulder. Advanced cases of the Eye of Mystra spellscar leaves the Spellsick disfigured. In very rare cases the corruption starts in both eyes or takes over the other eye over time.

#### Ability Score Improvement

Your Intelligence score is increased by 1.

#### Darkvision

Your corrupted eye enhances your eyesight past what was previously possible or back to what was once lost. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of blue.

#### Uncover Hidden Knowledge

Your corrupted eye reveals the secrets of the weave. You gain the ability to use both the Detect Magic and Identify spells once each without using material components or using a spellslot, you do not learn these spells. You can use these spells again after a long rest.

### Heart of Weave

The corruption of the spellsickness grows from your heart, you have a giant glowing hole in your chest and your veins glow magically. You are much more resilient against poisons as the magic clears your blood. Over time the corruption will consume your lungs and other internal organs and the glowing veins will spread across the whole body in a branching lightning scar pattern. Many spellsick attempt to cover the Heart of Weave when the corruption is young but over time embrace the appearance and judgement brought by it.

#### Ability Score Improvement

Your Constitution score is increased by 1.

#### Corrupted Blood

The Spellsickness claims dominance over your body and clears your blood of any poisons. You gain advantage on saving throws against Poison and Resistance to Poison damage.

#### Enchanted Physiology

You can push your body past it's natural limit. Your corrupted organs may eternally hurt and cause a sickened cough but they are empovered nontheless. You have advantage on Constitution saving throws against being exhausted. You also gain accelerated regeneration, you heal back your Constitution modifier's worth of Hit Points each hour that you are awake (to a minimum of 2).

### Limbs of Ether

The Corruption of the Spellsick has caused their Limbs to partially phase out and appear glowing and see through. Your feel lighter and your movements are quiet but your limbs ache constantly and Antimagic removes these boons and grinds you to a halt. In the beginning it may only be the fingers and toes and over time the limbs are consumed more and more, spreading the corruption and pain. The phantom limbs are almost weightless but still tangable and woundable. If you ever enter the Ethereal plane you will be revert back to a perfectly healthy Spellsickness free state for as long as you remain in the Ethereal plane. 

#### Ability Score Improvement

Your Dexterity score is increased by 1.

#### Weightless Limbs

Your arms and legs weigh almost nothing. When you are not wearing Medium or Heavy armor you gain 30 feet of climbing speed. When wearing Medium armor the climbing speed is reduced to 15 feet and when wearing Heavy armor the Climbing speed is reduced to 0.  
You can cast the Longstrider spell on yourself only once between long rests without using a spellslot.

#### Light Step

You gain Proficiency in Stealth.

### Mana Burst

_Prerequisites: Constitution 15 or higher_  
You've begun to tap into the wild magic that permeates your body, granting you the ability to weaponize it in short bursts of power, speed, or endurance.

-   You may choose between one of the following types of mana burst to learn:

**Burst of Strength** As a bonus action whenever you make a melee attack or Ability check using Strength, you may expend one hit die (without any modifiers) to add half (rounded up) of the result (to a minimum of 1) to your damage roll, accuracy check, or the Ability check roll. Additionally, you may also expend a hit die to count as one size larger for the amount you can push, drag, and lift for the next minute.

**Burst of Speed** As a bonus action whenever you make an Ability check using Dexterity you may expend one hit die (without any modifiers) to add half (rounded up) of the result (to a minimum of 1) to your roll. Additionally, you may also gain a sudden burst of speed whenever initiative is rolled allowing you to expend a hit die and add the result to your roll. Finally, once per round, you may gain a sudden burst of speed whenever you use your movement action, by expending a hit die you may move in any direction by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus times 5.

**Burst of Endurance** As a bonus action, you may expend a hit die brace for an incoming attack as you harden your body or armor if you are wearing any for the inevitable. Until the start of your next turn, you may add an amount equal to your proficiency bonus (rounded up) to any Strength, and Constitution saving throws and half of that amount (rounded up) to your AC. Additionally, as a reaction whenever you take damage, you may expend one hit die (without any modifiers) and reduce your damage taken by that amount.
