# Giggling Bracelet
*Wondrous Item, Common*

**Origins:** A travelling troupe.

**Description:** A golden bracelet with silly imagery.

**Mechanics:**
* **Fearless Humor:** You have **advantage on all saving throws against fear**.
* **Uncontrollable Laughter:** You have **disadvantage on all saving throws** against magic/effects that cause you to laugh uncontrollably.
