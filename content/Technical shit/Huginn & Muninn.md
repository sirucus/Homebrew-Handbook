Huginn & Muninn (5e Equipment)

Weapon (dagger), Rare (requires attunement)


These twin daggers are made of ebony steel with onyx pommels. Carved into each blade is the figure of a raven.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with these magic weapons.

While wielding one dagger, the user may throw the other as a bonus action, after which the user will instantly teleport to its location, catch it, and attack the nearest creature. If you hit with this attack, the dagger deals an extra 2d4 piercing damage. A flurry of raven feathers are left at the previous location.
