a retired assassin that was living peacefully in [[babblebrook]] until [[the burning of babblebrook]]. used to work for [[the church]], he was high ranked enough that he was essentially a right hand man to the pope. he committed many sins during his time but had truly believed it was for the greater good. once he lost his wife and young daughter to [[the church]] during the burning, a rage burned in his heart that is nearly impossible to extinguish. he killed and tortured church soldiers, learning that they thought they were culling a cult of the mad god. he eventually learned the real reason, the giant heartstone. enraged further that it was for greed and power, he has become a one man army trying to take out [[the church]], without mercy.

he carrys a flower that is stained with blood. as he become more and more corrupted and lost to rage the flower slowly becomes more and more red, until it is fully encompassed. once he is lost to rage, [[the party]] will no longer be able to redeem him.
