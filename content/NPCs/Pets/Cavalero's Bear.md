A bear that was summoned by the pulse of wild magic from Arnans tantrum.

it was summoned during their trial contract for the adventurers guild. they were clearing a library of boggles. the bear stood around until it was rushed by a boggle, in which case it started wiping out the other boggles that came near it.

after the boggles fled, [[Cavalero Thrax|cavalero]] approached the bear with a kind hand to try and calm it down and avoid having to put it down.
