Harrowhark nonagesimus was born in a small tribe in the middle of nowhere. She was the second born to the chief of the tribe and the head of the Cult of the Locked Tomb. Her older brother Val was 10 when she was born. He became her best friend and only companion. She was a devout subject who would routinely attend and perform rituals. Harrow was very intelligent and curious, eager to learn new things. She would read any book, scroll or tablet she could find. But one day her curiosity went too far. The cult of the locked tomb has very little to do with worshiping gods and much more to do with one belief. It was their holy oath to keep the locked tomb sealed. The locked tomb is located underground in her village and is said to hold a dark and ancient evil. As Harrow approached 10 years old she began to hear whispers, barely audible. She was never able to understand what they were telling her but she knew they were coming from the tomb. Her curiosity grew and grew until it became an obsession. Then she snapped. Late at night she snuck down. using her intelligence she unlocked the magic defenses and finally peered inside.

Her head exploded with thoughts and voices promising unparalleled intelligence. On the spot she learned of spells she hadn't even heard of. Immediately knowing her mistake she quickly shut the tomb and relocked it. The voices quieted but never fully ceased. Racked with guilt she decided to tell her parents what happened. The following events were performed so coldly it was as if her parents were replaced by machines. Her father gathered the tribe and explained what happened and that “the final ritual” was to be performed. At once the tribe gathered their ropes and departed for the nearby forest. They believe the only thing that would save their souls now was to take their own life. Ropes were hung and families were wiped out. The Nonagesimus family hung four ropes: mother, father, brother, daughter. Harrow, knowing what she had to do, followed her family willingly. But in the back of her head she knew that wasn't what she wanted. There was so much more to know! she knew because the voices told her. When the time came, Mother hung, Father hung, Brother hung,.... Harrow did not. Harrow sat helpless as her entire tribe was eradicated before her eyes, but she couldn't join them. The voices promised a new purpose. That night Harrow set out into the world with the only three possessions she held dear. A longsword her brother used, a necklace made of bone from her mother, and a ring from her father. For the past 8 years Harrow has searched for new knowledge and new power with nothing but the voices to keep her company.
