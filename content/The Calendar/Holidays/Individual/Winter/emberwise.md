[[Holidays|holiday]]
During Emberwise people exchange small tokens that are enchanted to help prevent them from getting small burns from the falling flames and wish them some good tiding for the year to come. When the flames begin to fall heavier the people all gather inside to eat drink and be merry. The reason they are indoors is because it rains fire for the next couple days and the taverns and wealthy homes get their shops enchanted by mages.
