[[Holidays|holiday]]
Known for her abundant gift of food, hospitality, home and hearth, the Feast of Teresita is common amongst poorer folk, often gone unnoticed by the wealthy and nobility of the lands. These feasts may seem paltry by high-class standard, but it has become tradition for those with very little to make more than enough for their family and neighbors on this day. The feasts tend to be comprised of 'bulk foods' (noodles and veggie wraps), and always have a distinct taste with their bitter side-dish for dipping. Ask anyone who has held a spot at a Feast of Teresita and they'll share the feeling of love, warmth, security and abundance, even when surrounded by squalor. That is what empowers this feast throughout the ages in communities that need it most.
