[[Holidays|holiday]]
The story goes that during the many wars waged by these city states, a hoarding mentality came about. To combat this, after one particularly savage siege-based war the city council enlisted the Merchant's guild to go around and collect all the junk people had hoarded for survival, as well as dropping their prices on hard to sell items. Essentially, it has evolved into a massive garage sale that attracts all sorts of collectors from around the region, as the city now allows non citizens to Yeet their items and take part. The slogan of the holiday states "if it can survive being yeeted, someone can sell it." The catch is that while Merchant collectors hunt around for items to sell, it becomes a game of who can find the best item the fastest. If you get to an item first, you can lay claim on it. However, you can only take one item from this event, and if you hold 2 you must choose one to Yeet.
