*Weapon (Any Axe or Hammer), Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Dwarven Battleragers.

**Mechanics:**
* **Ramp up:** Each time you damage a creature, gain a **+1 bonus** to attack/damage for subsequent attacks against that same creature (Max +3).
* **Reset:** Bonus ends if creature dies, you attack a different target, or 1 hour passes.
