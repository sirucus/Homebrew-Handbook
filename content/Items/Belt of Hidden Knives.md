*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Origins:** An unassuming black leather belt.

**Mechanics:**
* **Infinite Daggers:** As part of an attack, draw a hidden **+1 Dagger**.
* **Vanish:** The dagger ceases to exist at the end of the turn if it leaves your hand.
