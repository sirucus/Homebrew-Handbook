*Weapon (Halberd), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+3 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Light:** Bonus action to shed bright light (30ft) and dim light (30ft). Toggle off with bonus action.
