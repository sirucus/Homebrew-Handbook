*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Boots of animal skin with gem-studded soles.

**Mechanics:**
* **Speed:** Walking speed increases by 5ft.
* **Terrain:** Ignore difficult terrain based on skin type:
    * **Lizard:** Jungle, marsh, swamp.
    * **Deer:** Forest.
    * **Ram:** Mountains.
    * **Reindeer:** Snow, ice.
