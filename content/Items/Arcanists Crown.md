*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

**Origins:** A powerful leader of a witches' coven.

**Mechanics:**
* **Coven Power:** You gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and save DCs based on the number of allied spellcasters within 15ft of you: 1 ally (+1), 3 allies (+2), 5+ allies (+3).
* **Shared Bless:** If there are at least 3 allied spellcasters within 15ft, you gain the benefits of the *bless* spell (no concentration required).
