*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Origins:** A bizarre orc.

**Mechanics:**
* **Implant:** Attune by permanently replacing a tooth (remove only via Wish).
* **Activate:** 3 charges. Bonus action to activate effect + roll Wild Magic Surge:
    1.  **Heal:** Regain 3d8+4 HP.
    2.  **Roar:** Cast *Fear* (DC 13).
    3.  **Harden:** Resistance to acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder until next turn.
