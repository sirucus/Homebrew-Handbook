*Weapon (Any Spear), Common (Requires Attunement by Small/Smaller creature)*

**Origins:** Warlike Goblins.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charges:** 3 charges (regains at midnight).
* **Spite:** Hit Medium or larger creature: Expend 1 charge for extra **1d6 piercing**.
* **Retaliate:** Reaction (1 charge) when missed by Medium or larger creature to make a melee attack against them.
