*Weapon (Sickle), Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Desperate farmers dealing with fey.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage.
* **Reap:** Against Plant, Swarm, or Beast: Deal extra **1d4 slashing** and target has disadvantage on next attack.
* **Curse:** Must sacrifice a humanoid daily. If not, lose half Hit Dice and age 1 year (or attunement breaks).
