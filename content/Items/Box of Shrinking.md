*Wondrous Item, Rarity Varies*

**Origins:** Carved with Dwarvish runes; plays a nursery rhyme.

**Mechanics:**
* **Shrink:** Action to shrink one non-living object within 50ft (size limit varies by rarity) into a 1-inch cube inside the box. (1/Day).
* **Grow:** Action to restore a shrunken item to original size. (1/Day).
* **Capacity:**
    * **Iron (Uncommon):** 5ft cube.
    * **Steel (Rare):** 10ft cube.
    * **Mithral (Very Rare):** 15ft cube.
    * **Adamantine (Legendary):** 20ft cube.
