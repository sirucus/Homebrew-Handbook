*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Origins:** A powerful monastery dedicated to hunting down shadows.

**Mechanics:**
* **Hallow Immunity:** You are immune to the negative effects of the *hallow* spell.
* **Ghost Ward:** Incorporeal creatures (Wraiths, Ghosts, etc.) have disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
