*Weapon (Any Axe), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** An ancient northern tribe that once occupied Mount Nar.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Spells:** 3 charges (regains all at dusk). Cast using Charisma: *Bless* (1 charge) or *Speak with Dead* (2 charges).
* **Guardian:** If you have half HP or less, use an action to summon a Ghost (friendly). You command it via bonus action. Lasts 1 minute or until 0 HP. Recharges at dusk.
