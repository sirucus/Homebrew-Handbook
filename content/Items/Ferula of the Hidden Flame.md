*Staff, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Petrified plant stalk with scalding flowers.

**Mechanics:**
* **Wall of Fire:** Cast *Wall of Fire* (1/Day). Can be invisible and moved 20ft as bonus action.
* **Augury:** Cast *Augury* (1/Day). Staff ignites for weal, wilts for woe.
