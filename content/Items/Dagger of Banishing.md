*Weapon (Dagger), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Jade hilt depicting a solar banishing a fiend.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Sense:** Know location of portals within 100ft.
* **Banish:** Hit on extra-planar creature forces DC 15 Charisma save.
    * Fail: Banished to home plane for 1d4 days. (Cooldown: 1d4 days).
    * **Destroy:** Can choose to destroy dagger on success to banish for 10d10 years.
