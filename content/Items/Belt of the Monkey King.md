*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A long-forgotten kingdom of Maztica.

**Mechanics:**
* **Agility:** Your Dexterity score becomes **19** (no effect if already higher).
* **Tail:** Bonus action to manifest a prehensile tail. Can carry minor items. Grants advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) and climbing checks.
