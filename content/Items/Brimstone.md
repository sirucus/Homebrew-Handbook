*Weapon (Shoulder Cannon), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Fire Bolts:** Fires 1st-level *Fire Bolt* cantrips instead of cannonballs (Automatic property). Reloads after 24 shots (Action).
* **Fireball:** 3 charges (regains all on Long Rest). Expend 1 charge to cast *Fireball*.
* **Cooldown:** After casting *Fireball*, loses magic for 1 hour (fires mundane rounds dealing 1d6 piercing).
