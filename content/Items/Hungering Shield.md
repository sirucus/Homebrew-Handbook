*Shield, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Priests of Grolantor.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charges:** 3 charges (regains 1d2+1 at dawn).
* **Bite:** Bonus action (1 charge): Melee weapon attack (+5 to hit, **2d6+2 slashing**).
* **Curse:** Must be fed daily. If not, roll d20 on initiative. On 9 or lower, shield attacks you.
