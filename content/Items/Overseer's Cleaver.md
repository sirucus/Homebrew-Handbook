*Weapon (Any Axe), Uncommon (Requires Attunement by an evil creature)*

**Origins:** A cruel slaver.

**Mechanics:**
* **Torment:** Hitting a Frightened, Poisoned, or Paralyzed creature forces a DC 13 Con save.
* **Agony:** On fail, target takes **1d6 necrotic** per condition (blinded, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned) at start of their turns. Ends on unconsciousness, healing, or condition removal.
