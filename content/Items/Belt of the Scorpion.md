*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Mithral chain with a large emerald.

**Mechanics:**
* **Poison:** Resistance to poison damage; advantage on saves vs poison.
* **Stinger:** Action to grow a mithral tail.
    * **Attack:** Finesse, Martial, Reach. **+3 bonus** to attack/damage. Deals **1d6 piercing**.
    * **Venom:** On hit, can force DC 18 Con save. Fail = Paralyzed 1 minute. (1/Long Rest).
