*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Worshippers of a fallen tiger demon lord.

**Mechanics:**
* **Jungle King:** Cannot get lost in jungle; difficult terrain doesn't slow travel; alert to danger; travel stealthily at normal pace.
* **Tiger Claws:** Unarmed strikes become magic weapons dealing **1d8 slashing** damage with a **+2 bonus**.
* **Pounce:** If you move 20ft straight toward a creature and hit with an unarmed attack, target must pass DC 15 Str save or be knocked prone.
