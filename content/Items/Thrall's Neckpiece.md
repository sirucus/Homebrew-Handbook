*Wondrous Item, Common (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Cunning vampires.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charges:** 3 charges (regains at midnight).
* **Leech:** Hit with melee attack -> Reaction (1 charge) to deal extra **1d6 necrotic** and gain equal THP.
* **Curse:** Rolling a 1 on attack forces you to cast *Dissonant Whispers* on yourself (DC 13).
