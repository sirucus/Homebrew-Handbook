*Rod, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Mithral and moonstone with cage engravings.

**Mechanics:**
* **Banish:** Action to touch creature -> DC 15 Cha save or transport to Pandemonium. (Fail immunity 1d4 weeks).
* **Swap:** If another creature is trapped, banishing a new one swaps them.
* **Retrieve:** Action to bring trapped creature back.
