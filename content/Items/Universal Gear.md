*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A priestess of Gond.

**Mechanics:**
* **Mending:** Cast *Mending* (verbal only).
* **Integrate:** Attach to a machine using *Mending*. You gain knowledge of operation (drive vehicle, operate siege engine).
* **Mechanic:** If attached to lock/trap, advantage to open/disarm.
