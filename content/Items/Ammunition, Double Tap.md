*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Mechanics:**
* **Repeat:** When attacking, repeat the attack roll against the same creature at the start of your next turn (hit or miss).
* **Switch:** If the first target drops to 0 HP, the second attack can target a different creature within 30ft.
* **Expend:** Ammunition becomes mundane after both attacks are resolved.
