*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A chief dragon hunter.

**Mechanics:**
* **Resistance:** After a short or long rest, choose resistance to: lightning, poison, fire, or cold. Lasts until next rest.
* **Resilience:** Once per long rest, you may choose to succeed on a failed saving throw.
