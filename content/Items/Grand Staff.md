*Staff, Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Bard)*

**Origins:** White birch staff with grooves.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+3 bonus** to attack, damage, and spell attack rolls.
* **Charges:** 20 charges (regains 3d6+2 at dawn). Regains 1d6+2 on d20 roll of 20 when empty.
* **Constructive Interference:** Reaction (4 charges) to grant advantage on ally's spell attack or disadvantage on target's save.
* **Destructive Interference:** Reaction (5 charges) to cast 5th-level *Counterspell* (+3 check bonus).
* **Resonant Strike:** Expend 1 charge on hit for extra **1d8 thunder**.
* **Antiresonance:** After 3 Resonant Strikes on same target, action (5 charges) to deal **10d10 force damage** (DC 20 Con half).
* **Spells:** *Confusion* (4), *Dispel Magic* (5), *Otto's Irresistible Dance* (6), *Power Word Stun* (8), *Shatter* (2), *Telekinesis* (5), *Thunderwave* (1), *Wall of Force* (5).
