*Staff, Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a Cleric)*

**Origins:** Golden staff with dragon heads and crystal.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+2 bonus** to spell attack rolls. Resistance to necrotic/radiant.
* **Charges:** 10 charges (regains 2d4+2 at dawn).
* **Spells:** *Beacon of Hope* (3), *Bless* (1), *Ceremony* (1), *Dispel Evil and Good* (5), *Sanctuary* (1).
* **Temple:** Create *Temple of the Gods* (permanent after 100 years).
