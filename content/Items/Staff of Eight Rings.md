*Staff, Artifact (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Plain wooden staff with eight rings stolen from sorcerer kings.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+3 bonus** to attack and damage.
* **Channel:** Choose one Minor Power at dawn. Can use 3 Major Powers per day.
* **Fracture:** 20% chance for a ring to break when Major Power is used.
* **Rings:**
    * **Bone:** Minor (Gain THP equal to 2x damage dealt; target can't heal). Major (AoE **10d10 necrotic**; kills become shadows).
    * **Copper:** Minor (+2d6 force on hit; 1d6 force AoE). Major (Hit: **12d12 force** + 60ft push/prone. AoE: **6d6 force** + prone).
    * **Glass:** Minor (Hit causes Blindness, DC 18 Wis). Major (1 hour Invisibility + Illusions causing **8d8 psychic**).
    * **Gold:** Minor (Hit causes Frightened, DC 18 Wis). Major (AoE Frightened and Paralyzed, DC 20 Wis).
    * **Iron:** Minor (Spectral copy attacks second target). Major (Bonus action Teleport 60ft + *Gate* spell).
    * **Lead:** Minor (Hit removes reaction/movement, DC 18 Con). Major (Hit: **10d8 force**, reduce AC/Atk, destroy nonmagical gear).
    * **Opal:** Minor (Learn stats + track location). Major (Truesight 120ft + *Foresight*).
    * **Silver:** Minor (Dispel Magic 4th level+ on hit). Major (*Antimagic Field*).
