*Gauntlet, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A green Slaad monk.

**Mechanics:**
* **Strikes:** Unarmed strikes get a **+2 bonus** and are magical.
* **Chaos Form:** Bonus action to transform arm for 1d6 turns (1 charge, regains at dusk). Roll d4:
    1.  **Blade:** Acts as a one-handed Greatsword +2.
    2.  **Stone:** Unarmed damage becomes 1d10; advantage on grapple checks.
    3.  **Tongue:** 15ft reach, **2d8 acid damage**. Casters hit roll concentration at disadvantage.
    4.  **Choice:** Choose any form.
