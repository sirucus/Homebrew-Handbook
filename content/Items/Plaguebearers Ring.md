*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Mad cultists.

**Mechanics:**
* **Immunity:** Resistance to poison damage.
* **Explode:** 2 charges. When rolling a Death Save, expend 1 charge to deal **8d6 poison damage** to all within 30ft (DC 15 Dex half). You take a death save failure.
