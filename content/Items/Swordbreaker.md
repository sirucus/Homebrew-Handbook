*Weapon (Pump Action Shotgun), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+2 bonus** to attack/damage. **+2 AC**.
* **Counter:** Reaction to add +3 AC vs melee. If miss, make bayonet attack (**1d10 slashing**).
* **Bulltrue:** Reaction while dodging to shoot melee attacker. If they fail save, they are pushed 10ft, prone, and attack cancels.
