*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A clan of Long Death monks.

**Mechanics:**
* **Strikes:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage with unarmed strikes/natural weapons. Attacks are magical and can deal necrotic damage.
* **Siphon:** When you kill a hostile creature (CR 1+) with unarmed strikes, gain temporary hit points equal to **CR x 3**.
