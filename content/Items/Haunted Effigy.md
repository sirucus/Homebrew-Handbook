*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a non-good creature)*

**Origins:** Vengeful Drow necromancers.

**Mechanics:**
* **Totem:** Action to activate all placed statuettes within 120ft. Becomes Medium object (AC 16, 42 HP).
* **Aura:** Creatures within 10ft take **3d10 necrotic damage** (DC 14 Con save).
* **Raise:** Humanoids killed by aura rise as Zombies (friendly, but not under command).
