*Weapon (Any Pistol), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Vampiric:** Regenerate **10 HP/round** if not in sunlight. Gain Darkvision 120ft, *Spider Climb*, and Dex/Str/Con become 18 (if lower).
* **Drain:** Regain HP equal to half damage dealt.
* **Charm:** 1/Long Rest, charm creature (DC 15 Wis save). Lasts 24 hours.
* **Curse:** Must drink humanoid blood daily or gain exhaustion. Skin becomes pallid, canines grow. Killing humanoid forces DC 15 Wis save or compel to feast.
