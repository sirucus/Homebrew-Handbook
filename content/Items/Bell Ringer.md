*Weapon (Maul), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A glassteel head studded with rubies in the shape of a bell.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Thunder:** Deals extra **1d6 thunder damage**.
* **Shockwave:** Action to strike ground. Creatures within 20ft must pass DC 15 Dex save or take **4d8 thunder damage** and be knocked prone (half damage/no prone on success). (1/Long Rest).
