*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Silver monkeys with jade eyes.

**Mechanics:**
* **Climb:** Add double proficiency to Athletics (Climb) checks.
* **Tail:** Action to grow a prehensile tail for 1 hour (1/Long Rest).
    * Can hang from tail.
    * Acts as a third hand (manipulate objects up to 5 lbs).
