*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a humanoid proficient in water vehicles)*

**Origins:** A legendary pirate.

**Mechanics:**
* **Sea Legs:** Swim speed equals walking speed.
* **Master:** Double proficiency bonus on Wisdom (Survival) and Vehicles (Water) checks while waterborne.
* **Ward:** Reaction when taking fire damage: Immune to fire until next turn (1/Long Rest).
