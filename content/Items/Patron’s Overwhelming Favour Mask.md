*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Attack:** Add Cha mod to attack rolls.
* **Kill:** Killing blow grants **Level + Cha Mod** THP.
* **Senses:** Devil's Sight 30ft. Immune to Charm. No food/sleep.
* **Curse:** Cannot be removed (rips face off). Binds soul to patron.
