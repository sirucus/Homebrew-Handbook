*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Origins:** Copper orb dotted with obsidians.

**Mechanics:**
* **Swarm:** Throw to create 500 copper beetles.
* **Path:** Beetles clear a 5ft wide path through difficult terrain (vegetation/snow). Lasts 10 hours. (1/Dawn).
