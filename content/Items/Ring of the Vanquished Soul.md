*Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Black band with a skull-shaped ruby; emits moans.

**Mechanics:**
* **Absorb:** Killing a creature absorbs a soul piece.
* **Release:** Expend soul to gain Advantage on one check, attack, or save.
* **Found:** 50% chance to start with a soul.
