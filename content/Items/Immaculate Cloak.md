*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Origins:** A mage driven mad by the cold.

**Mechanics:**
* **Purity:** Immunity to disease, poison, and poison damage.
* **Curse:** You suffer symptoms of a cold and gain Indefinite Madness (Hypochondria). Requires *Remove Curse* to doff.
