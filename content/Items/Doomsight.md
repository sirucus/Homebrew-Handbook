*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** An overly generous beholder.

**Mechanics:**
* **Vision:** Darkvision 120ft.
* **Eye Rays:** 4 charges (regains 1d4 at dawn). Bonus action to fire a random ray (d4):
    1.  **Fear:** DC 13 Wis save or frightened 1 minute.
    2.  **Death:** DC 13 Con save or **3d10 necrotic** (half on success).
    3.  **Force:** DC 15 Str save or pushed 30ft.
    4.  **Illusion:** DC 13 Int save or see walls in all directions.
