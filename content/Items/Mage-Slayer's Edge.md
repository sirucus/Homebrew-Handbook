*Weapon (Light Slashing), Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Assassin guild.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage.
* **Disrupt:** Hit applies a Spell-curse stack. If target casts a spell, they roll d20. If result <= stacks, spell fails. Stacks clear on rest.
* **Curse:** Using a potion or scroll requires a d4 roll. On a 1, item fails.
