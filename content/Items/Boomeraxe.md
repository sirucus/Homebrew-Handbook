*Weapon (Handaxe), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Jade-studded steel haft with adamantine heads.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Return:** Returns to hand when thrown.
* **Fly:** Action to throw in 60ft line (3/Day).
    * Creatures in line make Dex save (DC 8 + Str + Prof).
    * Fail: **6d6 slashing** (Half on success).
    * Returns along the same line next turn (repeating damage).
