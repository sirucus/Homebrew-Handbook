*Weapon (Simple: Slashing or Piercing), Common (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A bloodthirsty tribe.

**Mechanics:**
* **Magical:** Attacks count as magical.
* **Finisher:** When you damage a target with 10 HP or less, you can choose to deal an extra **1d10 psychic damage** to both yourself and the target.
* **Vitality:** When you kill a creature, gain **1d10 temporary hit points**.
* **Curse:** Cannot deal non-lethal damage.
