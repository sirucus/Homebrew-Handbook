*Weapon (Lever Action Rifle), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Force:** Deals extra **1d4 force damage**.
* **Barrage:** 4 charges (regains 1d4 on long rest).
    * **1 Charge:** Cast *Conjure Barrage*.
    * **2 Charges:** Cast *Conjure Volley*.
    * **Control:** Can exclude creatures equal to **2 + Dex/Wis mod**.
