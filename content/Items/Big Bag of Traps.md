*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Origins:** A kobold inventor.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charges:** 4 charges (regains 1d4 at dawn).
* **Deploy:** Action (1 charge) to throw a token 20ft. After 1 minute, it transforms into a trap. After another minute, it camouflages (DC 15 spot/disarm).
* **Random Trap:** Roll d4 (Kobolds can choose):
    1.  **Spring-Loaded Net:** Restrains medium/smaller creatures in 10ft square.
    2.  **Flaming Disc:** Explodes when approached (5ft). 4d6 fire damage (15ft radius, DC 13 Dex half).
    3.  **Bag of Bugs:** Swarm of insects emerges and attacks triggerer.
    4.  **Lightning Rod:** Releases charge when approached (5ft). 3d10 lightning damage (DC 13 Dex half).
