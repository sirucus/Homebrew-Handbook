*Race: Multidron*

**Origins:** Handmade Modrons from Mechanus.

**Mechanics:**
* **ASI:** Int +1, Con +1.
* **Type:** Construct (Living). Affected by healing spells.
* **Resilience:** Advantage vs Poison saves, resistance to poison damage. Immune to disease.
* **Nature:** No need for food/water/sleep/breath.
* **Fuel:** Must consume fuel (wood, water, gold, acid, flesh, etc.) daily or suffer exhaustion.
* **Gimmick:** Choose one: Extended Reach, Cantrip, Skill Expertise, +10ft Speed, Elemental Resistance, or 1d4 Finesse Unarmed Strike.
