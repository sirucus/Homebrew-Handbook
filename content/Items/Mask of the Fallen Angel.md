*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Magic:** Cast *Chill Touch*. Resistance to Necrotic. Dims light.
* **Curse:** Healing spells damage you. Use *Chill Touch* to heal yourself/undead/evil (1d8+Mod).
* **Screech:** When hit by healing/radiant, can double damage to trigger Banshee Screech (Con save: Fail = Half HP/Deaf; Success = 3d6 psychic).
