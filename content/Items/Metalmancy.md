*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Bard)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Form:** Metallic lute that doubles as a **+2 Repeating Rifle** (Uses Charisma).
* **Ammo:** Fires bardic energy (**1d6 thunder** + **1d6 fire**). Infinite ammo; reload by playing powerchord.
* **Thunderstruck:** During Countercharm, can make spell attacks at two creatures (4d6 lightning).
* **Dragonforce:** Casting fire/thunder/lightning spell creates dragon illusion. Enemies must pass Wis save or be frightened.
* **Spells:** Charges (4). Cast *Thunderwave*, *Fireball*, *Control Weather*.
