*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A heretical Drow cult that worships Driders.

**Mechanics:**
* **Friendship:** Use an action to cast *Animal Friendship* (DC 13), targeting only spiders.
* **Spells:** 1 charge (regains at dusk). Cast *Darkness*, *Faerie Fire*, or *Web* (DC 13).
