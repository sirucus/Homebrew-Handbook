*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A powerful lich.

**Mechanics:**
* **Undying:** You automatically succeed on Death Saving Throws. Taking damage at 0 HP does not cause failures.
* **Vulnerability:** You can still die from massive damage or environmental effects (e.g., suffocation).
* **Curse:** For every hour attuned, your natural lifespan decreases by one day. If you die of old age while wearing it, you rise as a zombie.
