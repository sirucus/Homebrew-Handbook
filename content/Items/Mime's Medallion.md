*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A bard of charades.

**Mechanics:**
* **Silent Magic:** Spells require no verbal components.
* **Cantrips:** Cast *Silent Image* and *Minor Illusion* at will (no material).
* **Spells:** 5 charges (regains at dawn). Cast using Charisma: *Disguise Self* (1), *Colour Spray* (1), *Blur* (2), *Phantasmal Force* (2).
* **Grease:** Bonus action (1 charge) to leave a 5ft wide grease trail for a turn.
* **Curse:** You cannot speak. You deal 0 thunder damage.
