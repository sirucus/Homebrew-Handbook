*Race: Animite*

**Origins:** A soul trapped in a manufactured body.

**Mechanics:**
* **ASI:** Con +1.
* **Type:** Construct (Living). Affected by healing spells.
* **Nature:** No need for food/water/sleep/breath.
* **Memento:** Must possess a specific item. If lost, disadvantage on death saves.
* **Subraces:**
    * **Artisan:** Ability +1, Tool Expertise, *Mending* cantrip.
    * **Companion:** Cha +1, Persuasion/Deception prof, Audio Mimicry.
    * **Knight:** Str or Dex +1, +1 AC, Weapon Proficiency.
