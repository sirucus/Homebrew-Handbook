*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Death worshippers.

**Mechanics:**
* **Undead Nature:** No need for air, food, drink, or sleep.
* **Cost:** Removing mask causes 1 level of exhaustion.
* **Curse:** After 1 year, it fuses to your face. You become immune to healing spells.
