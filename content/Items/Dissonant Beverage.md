*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Origins:** A Wild Mage mixologist.

**Mechanics:**
* **Regenerate:** Creates a new liquid daily. Mundane wine 1 minute after opening.
* **Analyze:** DC 25 Alchemist/Brewer/Nature check to identify effect.
* **Effect (d6):**
    1.  Heal 3d8+3 HP.
    2.  Gain Short Rest benefits, then 1 level of exhaustion.
    3.  Cure Madness, then fall unconscious for 1d6+6 hours.
    4.  Set ablaze (Alchemist's Fire effect).
    5.  Poisoned (Pale Tincture effect).
    6.  Take 3d12+6 Cold damage (DC 15 Con).
