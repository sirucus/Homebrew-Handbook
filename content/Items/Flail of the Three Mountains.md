*Weapon (Flail), Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Artisans of the Great Dismal Delve.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+2 bonus** to attack and damage.
* **Force:** Deals extra **3d4 force damage**.
* **Mode:** Choose one head after a Long Rest:
    * **Stone:** Reduce forced movement by 15ft.
    * **Sand:** Reaction to halve slashing/piercing/bludgeoning damage.
    * **Mud:** Immune to poison and disease.
