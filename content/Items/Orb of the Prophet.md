*Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

**Origins:** A fortune-teller.

**Mechanics:**
* **Divination:** 3 charges (regains 1d3 at dawn). Cast *Commune*, *Contact Other Plane*, or *Legend Lore* (1 charge, Wisdom based).
* **Fate:** Reaction + 3 charges to change a visible creature's d20 roll to a **Natural 20**.
