*Staff, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Ironwood stick with a red scarab trapped in amber.

**Mechanics:**
* **Sand Form:** Bonus action to become swirling sand (1 minute).
    * **Movement:** Fly and Burrow speed equal to walking speed (double burrow if in sand). Move through 1-inch spaces.
    * **Resist:** Immune to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing.
    * **Limit:** No attacks/spells while in form. Cooldown 1d8-1 days.
