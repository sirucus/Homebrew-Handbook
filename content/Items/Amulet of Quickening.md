*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Clerics of Akadi.

**Mechanics:**
* **Speed:** Your movement speed is doubled.
* **Charges:** 5 charges (regains 1d4+1 daily at dusk).
* **Haste:** Action (1 charge): Cast *Haste* (no material components required).
* **Quickened Spell:** When you cast a spell with a casting time of one action, you can expend 1 charge to change the casting time to one bonus action.
* **Flurry:** When you take the Attack action, you can expend up to 3 charges to make an additional attack for each charge expended.
