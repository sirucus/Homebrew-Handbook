*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A tribe of tundral barbarians.

**Mechanics:**
* **Affinity:** Advantage on Charisma and Animal Handling checks regarding mammoths and elephants.
* **Stomp:** 2 charges (regains 1d2 at dawn). When you knock a creature prone within 5ft, bonus action (1 charge) to stomp. Target takes **2d10+3 bludgeoning damage** (DC 13 Dex save negates).
