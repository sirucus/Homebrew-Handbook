*Weapon (Any Firearm), Legendary (Requires Attunement by Wizard or Gunslinger)*

**Origins:** Contains the soul of a friendly dog.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+3 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Sentient:** Int 6, Wis 20, Cha 16. Communicates in barks/emotions.
* **Senses:** Auto-rolls Perception/Insight. Can sniff out invisible/ethereal creatures (30ft).
* **Spells:** Casts *Find Traps* and *Hunter's Mark* (5th level) at will.
* **Guard:** Acts in combat (initiative before user). Can Help (grant d4 bonus).
