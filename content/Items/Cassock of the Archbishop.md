*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*

**Origins:** An archbishop of war.

**Mechanics:**
* **AC:** If unarmored, AC is **15 + Dex modifier**. Can be worn over armor.
* **Healer:** Healing spells (1st level+) heal additional HP equal to Wisdom modifier.
* **Harm:** Necromancy damage spells (1st level+) deal additional damage equal to Wisdom modifier.
* **Restriction:** Color must match alignment (Red/Evil, Green/Neutral, Blue/Good).
