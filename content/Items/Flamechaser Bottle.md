*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Origins:** A genie warlord.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charges:** 3 charges (regains 1d3 at sunrise if touched by sun).
* **Extinguish:** Expend 1 charge to extinguish all magical and normal flames within 30ft.
* **Banish:** Fire elementals in range must pass DC 15 Charisma save or be banished.
