*Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A warlike clan of Yuan-ti.

**Mechanics:**
* **Serpent Strike:** You gain a **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with unarmed strikes and natural weapons. Such attacks are considered magical.
* **Constrict:** When you attack a Large or smaller creature and roll a **20** on the attack roll, you may grapple the target (escape DC 14). Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained and you cannot grapple another target.
