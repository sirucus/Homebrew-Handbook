*Staff, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Ancient bronze staff reminiscent of a shepherd's crook with a bronze bell.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **Charges:** 3 charges (regains 1d3+1 daily at midnight).
* **Sonorous Strike:** Expend 1 charge on hit. Creatures in 60ft radius must pass DC 16 Wisdom save or take **1d8 necrotic** (1d12 if below max HP). Target automatically fails.
* **Death Knell:** Action (3 charges) to strike ground. Creatures in 60ft radius must pass DC 16 Wisdom save or take **5d6 necrotic** (half on success). Fail causes Frightened status for 1 minute.
