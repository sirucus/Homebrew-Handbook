*Staff, Rare (Requires Attunement by Nature Cleric or Druid)*

**Origins:** Stick of petrified vines.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage.
* **Reach:** Attack at range (as *Thorn Whip*).
* **Spells:** Cast *Barkskin*, *Entangle*, or *Spike Growth* (1/Day).
* **Alarm:** Writhes near aberrations/undead.
* **Weakness:** *Blight* spell disables magic for 1d6 days.
