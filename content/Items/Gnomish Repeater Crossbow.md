*Weapon (Any Crossbow), Rare*

**Origins:** A gnome engineer.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** **+1 bonus** to attack and damage.
* **Burst:** 3 charges (regains 1d2+1 at dusk). Bonus action (1 charge): Roll 1d4 and make that many attacks.
* **Malfunction:** If the d4 roll is a 1, trigger a Wild Magic Surge.
