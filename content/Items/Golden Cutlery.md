*Wondrous Item (Set), Uncommon*

**Origins:** A halfling wizard chef.

**Mechanics:**
* **Spoon:** 3 charges. Cast *Create or Destroy Water* or *Detect Poison/Disease* (1 charge).
* **Fork:** 3 charges. Cast *Goodberry* (1 charge); can choose food appearance.
* **Knife:** Dagger (+1). Bonus action to change into Shortsword, Longsword, or Greatsword.
* **Set:** Attune to all three to gain Expertise in Cook's utensils and Brewer's supplies.
