*Staff, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** Stone-like spike.

**Mechanics:**
* **Shadow:** Action to connect shadow to creature's shadow.
* **Paralyze:** Target is paralyzed (frozen in time) until end of next turn (1/Day).
* **Cooldown:** Staff casts no shadow for 1d12 days after use.
