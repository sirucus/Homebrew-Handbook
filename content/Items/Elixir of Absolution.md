*Potion, Legendary*

**Origins:** A sinner seeking forgiveness.

**Mechanics:**
* **Erasure:** Single use. When consumed, the entire universe forgets the user exists (except the user).
* **Effect:** Documents are falsified, crimes explained as accidents, family forgets you. You become a "nameless wanderer."
