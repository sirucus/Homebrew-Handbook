*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A mad gnome.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charge:** 1 charge (regains at dawn).
* **Fire:** Action to charge (Speed becomes 0). Next turn, fires a 300ft long, 15ft wide cylinder of force.
* **Damage:** **20d10 force damage** (Dex save half). Disintegrates if reduced to 0 HP.
