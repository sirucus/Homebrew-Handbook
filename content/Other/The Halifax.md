![[grey_warden_s_ship_by_matmoura_daxyrge-fullview.jpg]]the sailing ship that [[Party 1]] commandered during [[The Burning of Babblebrook]].

it is currently captained by [[Dianna]]

after sailing to [[Valeris]], [[Dianna]] was bullying the dockworkers when suddenly the was a flash of blue light from the deck. [[Gray Retsub|gray]] had decided that the steering wheel of the halifax belonged in his hoard, and [[Arnan Jamjar|arnan]] assisted by sledgehammering the steering wheel off. this caused the wheel to come off and release a fair bit of ether into arnans face, temporarily blinding him and causing his eyes to glow a bright teal.

[[Dianna]] noticed that they had done this and demanded the wheel back or compensation for it. when [[Gray Retsub|gray]] tried to run off with it, she shot the steering wheel that was tied to his back causing it to slam into the back of his head knocking him down. she took the wheel back but [[Gray Retsub|gray]] noticed he had a sliver of wood from the wheel still in his lap.
