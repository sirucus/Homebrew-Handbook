*Wondrous Item, Artifact*

**Mechanics:**
* **Focus:** Can be used as a spellcasting focus; spells count as two levels higher than the slot used.
* **Unstable:** Physical damage causes a violent blast of radiant damage (equal to damage taken) in a 20ft radius.
* **Vulnerability:** Takes double damage from necrotic damage (does not trigger blast).
* **Destroyed by:** * If reduced to 0 HP by physical damage (100 HP total), it explodes dealing **10d10 radiant damage** in a 300ft sphere.
    * If reduced to 0 HP by necrotic damage, it shrivels to dust.
