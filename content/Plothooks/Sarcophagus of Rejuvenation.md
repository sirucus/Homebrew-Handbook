*Wondrous Item, Artifact*

**Mechanics:**
* **Heal:** Short rest restores all HP/cures disease. 8 hours regenerates limbs/youth.
* **Corruption:** Using without injury drains humanity (shifts alignment to chaotic evil).
* **Destroyed by:** Place a legendary item intended to cause harm inside, then destroy with a magic weapon.
