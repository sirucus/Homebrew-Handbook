*Wondrous Item, Artifact*

**Mechanics:**
* **Craft:** Weapons crafted with its knowledge deal double damage dice.
* **Cost:** Weapon must be soaked in ancient red dragon blood annually.
* **Defense:** Heals from damage and conjures spectral weapon to retaliate.
* **Destroyed by:** Burning in the forge where its first weapon was created.
