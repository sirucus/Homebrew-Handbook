*Wondrous Item, Artifact (Requires Attunement)*

**Mechanics:**
* **Fuse:** Fuses with user's heart (Unattuning kills user).
* **Logic:** User slowly loses emotion, becoming logic-centered.
* **Power:** Advantage on death saves. Portal to Mechanus (1/Day). Modrons are neutral.
* **Destroyed by:** Taint it in a plane of chaos, then destroy as normal object.
