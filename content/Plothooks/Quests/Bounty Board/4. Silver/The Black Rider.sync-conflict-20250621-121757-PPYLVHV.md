	Legend speaks of a rider wreathed in flame that once a month, on the eve of the waning crescent moon, appears along the Kragthi Path. I didn't believe the stories until I saw the man with my own two eyes - before it cleaved by arm clean from my body. I escaped, but Ifear my luck may soon run out, as each night I hear the cloven hoofs echoing from the trading roads. Iam looking for a group of adventurers willing to stop this waking nightmare, before I become another story to unwary travellers. Will pay in gold for proof of the rider's defeat. Find Taril Karyen at his home west of the Winter Marches and north of the Kragthi Path for more information. 

### Quest Details 
#### Level 8-10
Taril has become the target of a Narzugon (MKToF,pg 167), which patrols the Kragthi Path and the surrounding areas twice each night in search of him. The Narzugon is actively hostile to all travellers that pass it during the night. It patrols the roads between the hours of 12am to 3am, before disappearing, leaving behind a set of tracks burned into the dirt the Narzugon can use to lead trackers into an ambush. 

Rewards
600gp, 10pp, a nightcap that, when worn, gives you pleasant dreams, and an old slightly burned divination card bearing the likeness of one of the group.
