	A horrible stench is drifting across the hills from the north. A stench of decay, death and rot. Those that have scouted the terrain found nothing but blooming flowers amongst vibrant fields. Anyone that can find the source of this miserable odour will be well rewarded Speak with Harriet Ramshingle, at the Western Road Inn, if you would like to know more.

### Quest Details 
#### Level 10-11
The odour is originating from 2 Corpse Flowers(MKToF, pg 127) which have taken up residence outside the town, and are killing the local wildlife, before moving on to the locals. If left unchecked, they will assault the village and slaughter any they find

### Rewards 
1,230sp, 400gp and 10pp, awarded by Harriet on the destruction of both Corpse Flowers.
