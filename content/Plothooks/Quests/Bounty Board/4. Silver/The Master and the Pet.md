	A travelling salesmen is extorting our village by forcing us to hand over all our home-grown produce. He's exporting our goods to another region, and we aren't seeing a single penny from it! Problem is, he has this gigantic creature as a pet that's as loyal as a dog, and as ferocious as a charging manticore. Nobody here has the strength to stand up to such a beast.If anyone is willing to relieve us of this horrible man, you can find me at the Wingless Dove in Oakthistle, south of Rainymill Village. Ask for Lagazi. 

### Quest Details 
#### Level 9
The travelling merchant is known as Azzak Redmayne, who has set up business in Rainymill Village. He is a non- combatant, and has at his command a Gray Render (MKToF, pg 209, Quirk 2) which he is using to bully the villagers into doing his bidding. 
The Gray Render will defend it's master ferociously, and seems to be far more aggressive and feral than it's kin. Once the Gray Render is defeated, the villagers will swarm Azzak and either kill him, imprison him, or run him out of Rainymill 

### Rewards
2,000gp, a bag of Rainymill produce (counts as a month's rations between 4 people) a potion of Greater Healing (DMG, pg 187) and a horse and wagon, should the party require it.
