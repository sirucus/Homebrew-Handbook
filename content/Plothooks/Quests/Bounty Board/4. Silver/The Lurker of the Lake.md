	Fildo Kettlewhistle is convinced there is something lurking in Lake Takita. So far the crazy sailor has yet to supply any evidence, other than the half sunken remains of his less then seaworthy fishing boat. Kettlewhistle is insisting that I organise a search party for the 'monster'. For further information, contact Captain Ludwig in the Seafarer's Office, south of Lake Takita. Fildo is offering a small reward to anyone willing to fix his little problem. 

### Quest Details 
#### Level 9-10
Fildo is correct in his theory - Lake Takita has become the feeding ground for a Neothelid (VGtM, pg 181) The Neothelid has made its nest in an underground lake, located in the upper levels of the underdark, and is swimming to the surface using a network of excavated tunnels leading to the bed of Lake Takita. 

### Rewards
Upon providing proof of the creature's demise, Fildo will offer l,000cp, 552sp, and 300gp as a reward In addition, he will offer the following items, dredged up from the lake bed from his fishing trips: A sheet of parchment upon which is drawn a complex mechanical contraption and a tiny chest, carved to look like it has numerous feet on its bottom.
