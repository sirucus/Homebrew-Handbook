### Quest Details 
#### Level 5
Torog, the Half-Ore, is having a serious problem with a small group of Duergar, who are raiding his mining camp at night. The Duergar are stealing mining equipment and killing any guards that stand in their way. The group isled by a Duergar Mind Master (MKToF, pg 189) and a is composed of 3 other Duergar Stone Guards (MKToF, pg 191)

### Rewards 
The party will gain access to a horse and cart for travel and will be awarded 350cp and 800sp for their efforts.
