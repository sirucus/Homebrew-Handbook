	Horrific winged creatures are plaguing our once vibrant forest town! The swarm strikes at night, infecting our residents with a terrible curse that creates more of their ilk. Anyone willing to rid us of this monstrous infestation should speak with Holly Winterbane. You can find her taking refuge in the Shimmerwood Inn. 

### Quest Details
#### Level 6
The town is being plagued by a swarm of 10 Vargouilles (VGtM, pg 195) The swarm appears each night, between the hours of 10pm and 3am, to feed Any humanoids outside during this time will be targeted by the swarm. 

### Rewards
1,200cp, 800sp, and 300gp upon the successful eradication of all the Vargouilles, awarded by Holly Winterbane.
