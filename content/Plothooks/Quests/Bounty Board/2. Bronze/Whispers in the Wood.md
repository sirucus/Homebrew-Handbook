	A monstrous giant has been seen wandering the forests near Wellspring Hollow. This particular creature appears to be rather ancient, and highly aggressive. There is a l,500gp reward for anyone willing to slay the beast and give me it's location of death. For more information, contact Jaxi Kimbatuul at Prophecy Station.
