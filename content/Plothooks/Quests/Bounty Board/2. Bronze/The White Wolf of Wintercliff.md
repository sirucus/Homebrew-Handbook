	A gigantic wolf lives close to the iced fjord, just south of Wintercliff. The first person to slay the beast and bring me it's fangs will earn a hearty reward. You can find me at the Travelling Tarrasque Inn. Ask for Sinclair.

### Quest Details 
#### Level 5
There is a Winter Wolf (MM, pg 340) leading a pack of 10 Wolves (MM, pg 341). The winter wolf is intelligent enough to plan ambushes, and will hunt and attack any caravan passing through the woods that has food on it. It is also very territorial, and as such the pack will hunt out and attack any trespassers inside it's domain, which is a 5-mile radius from the pack's cave.

### Rewards 
184sp, and 103gp, awarded by Sinclair.
