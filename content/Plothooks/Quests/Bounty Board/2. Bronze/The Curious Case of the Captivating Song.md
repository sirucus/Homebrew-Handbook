### Quest Details 
#### Level 5
Armin has been hearing the mournful singing of a pair of ghosts (MM, pg 147) The ghosts are spirits of two people caught in the mine shaft collapse , and are trying to entice creatures inside to possess them and escape to wreak havoc. 

### Rewards
80cp, 40sp, 50gp, and an elven steel dragon ornament, stamped with a series of cryptic symbols, awarded by Armin.
