### Quest Details 
#### Level 6-7
Tenena promises roughly 30% of the vaults worth. The vault is being guarded by a Canoloth (MKToF, pg 247) which will remain protecting the vault and it's treasure until slain. Tena will join the party, but is a non combatant. Once slain, her reward will be dividing the loot of the vault between herself and the party, detailed below.
