### Quest Details 
#### Level 6
The town is being plagued by a Howler (MKToF, pg 210) which has found its way into the Material Plane from Pandemonium, and begun establishing a small lair in a cavern within the nearby woodland It will actively hunt for livestock and other beasts between the hours of 11pm and 3am, before leaving the carcass in a ravine nearby and retreating to its lair to rest. Carcasses are never left more than 1,000 feet from the cave entrance, and are rarely complete.
