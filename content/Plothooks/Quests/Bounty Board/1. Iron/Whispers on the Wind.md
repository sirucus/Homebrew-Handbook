	Embershrine Church has been having strange high winds lately that are causing damage to the surrounding areas, forcing many creatures to attempt to take shelter within the confines of the church grounds.Any adventurers willing to find the source of this problem should speak with Father Tye or Sister Shautha for more information.

### Quest Details 
#### Level 4
The area surrounding the church is being assailed by an Air Elemental (MM, pg 124). It will attack anything nearby, and will actively do so until slain.

### Rewards 
30sp, 14gp, and wooden box, engraved with the chapel's religion (Dm's choice) given by Sister Shautha.
