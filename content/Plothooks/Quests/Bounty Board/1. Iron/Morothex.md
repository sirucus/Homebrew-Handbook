### Quest Details 
#### Level 4
Diamondbridge has become the feeding ground for a Tlincalli (VGtM, pg 193), which is attempting to establish a new tribe in the caves nearby the settlement. The tlincalli has left it's tribe in order to prove its worth as a leader. 
If it continues to kill without consequence, more tlincalli will eventually be drawn to its proven might, and a tribe will be formed 
The tlincalli has taken two prisoners hostage, named Aloro and Makoto, whom are both the same race. Makoto is a murderer, and Aloro is a simple thief, though the party will not be informed who committed what crime when taking the bounty. Aloro will attempt to help the party, but will try to steal blades from them. Makoto will remain co-operative with the group, but won't hesistate to kill someone if she can escape. 

### Rewards
150gp, awarded by Captain J'alsei upon proof of the tlincalli's death. The group will earn an additional 75gp for returning the thief, and 120gp for returning the murderer.
