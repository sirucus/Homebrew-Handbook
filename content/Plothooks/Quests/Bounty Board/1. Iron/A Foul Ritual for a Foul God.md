	Wirry Sunmeadow has reported that her dog has been kidnapped by strange blue gnomes. The gnomes have been stealing from farmers and have even attempted to burn down 
	wheat field during this past week, so this has come as no surprise to anyone. Adventurers willing to hunt down these gnomes, recover Wirry’s pet and put an end to their problematic behaviour should contact Captain Yassa at the Oldspell Barracks for more information.

### Quest Details
#### Level 3
The town of Oldspell has been the target for a group of Xvarts (VGtM, pg 199) who have taken Wirry's dog to enact a ritual to Raxivort. The group will have 2 days to track and locate the Xvart's encampment before the dog is sacrificed The Xvart tribe consists of the following: 

10 Xvarts
1 Xvart Warlock of Raxivort, who also functions as the tribe's speaker, and can speak Goblin.

### Rewards
50sp and 15gp. awarded by Captain Yassa.
