### Quest Details 
#### Level 3
There is a Vampiric Mist (MKToF, pg 248) that is lurking in the fog that drifts into Backwater Cove twice a week, attacking any creatures in the fog at the time. Evan managed to escape, but has been badly wounded and has taken cover in a cave, blocking up the entrance to prevent the mist from 
entering. 

### Rewards
40cp and 20sp, awarded by Petri upon the safe return of Evan. Upon killing the Vampiric Mist, he will also give the party 2 potions of healing (DMG, pg 187)
