	A mechanical creature has been reported to be lurking in the battlefield near the western swamps. Survivors of both sides tell stories of a creature clad in armour of the dead with horrific glowing eyes. Anyone wishing to investigate these claims further should speak with Carrow Oakhand at the Western Watchtower. 

### Quest Details 
#### Level 12
A Cadaver Collector (MKToF, pg 124) has been sighted amassing corpses of a recent battle. It will attack any creature that comes near it, aiming to collect their corpse for it's pile. 

### Rewards
1,000sp, 750gp and 24pp, awarded by Carrow Oakhand on the successful banishment or radication of the cadaver collector.
