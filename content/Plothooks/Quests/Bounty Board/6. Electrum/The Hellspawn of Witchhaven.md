	Citizens of Witchhaven reported a rather gruesome scene unfold in the marketplace about a week ago. Something to do with a monstrous green looking creature exploding forth out of a man's chest, then fleeing into the sewers. Anyone wishing to investigate this further should contact Takeo Vezera, at the Witchhaven Barracks. 

### Quest Details 
#### Level 14-15
The town has a Slaadi infestation in it's sewers. There are 3 Red Slaad and 1 Blue Slaad led by a Death Slaad (MM, pg 276-278) The Slaadi have been kidnapping the residents of the town, and using them as incubators for more Slaadi. 

### Rewards
Upon clearing out the Slaadi infestation, Takeo will award the party 2,000sp, 400gp and 102pp, from the town coffers.
