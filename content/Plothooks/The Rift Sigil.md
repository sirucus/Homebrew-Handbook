*Wondrous Item, Artifact*

**Mechanics:**
* **Void:** Creates a hole that expands 1d10 feet weekly, consuming/destroying objects and creatures.
* **Travel:** Creatures can willingly enter (taking psychic damage) without being consumed.
* **Destroyed by:** Defeating the sentient "Rift Sigil Rune" entity inside the rift.
