*Rod, Artifact*

**Mechanics:**
* **Power:** Grants 10 additional spell slots. Spells cast have wild, evil effects.
* **Curse:** Wraps around the wrist permanently. Connects mind to an unknowable deity, corrupting the user.
* **Destroyed by:** Cutting off the bound arm and burning it along with the rod (requires sacrifice/purification to break mental link).
