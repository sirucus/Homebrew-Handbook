*Wondrous Item, Artifact*

**Mechanics:**
* **Unlock:** Opens any door.
* **Create Door:** Creates a door to anywhere, including parallel rooms for observation or other realities.
* **Destroyed by:** Opening a parallel room (where interaction is impossible), leaving the key inside, and closing the door.
