### Ranks

Using a crystal that can measure the density of the Aether in a person, the guild has found a pretty consistant way to measure someone's capabilities. By placing their hand on the crystal, an adventurer can have themselves measured and placed into the guilds different ranks
| Rank     | Color      | Level |
| -------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Copper   | Maroon     | 1     |
| Iron     | Red        | 3     |
| Bronze   | Orange     | 5     |
| Steel    | Yellow     | 7     |
| Silver   | Green      | 9     |
| Gold     | Teal       | 11    |
| Electrum | Blue       | 13    |
| Platinum | Purple     | 15    |
| Diamond  | White      | 17    |
| Mithral  | Unreadable | 19    |
