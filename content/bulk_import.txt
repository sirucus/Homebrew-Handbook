# Doomsight
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** An overly generous beholder.

**Description:** A highly exquisite monocle made of gold and silver. It grants some of the power that beholders are capable of invoking.

**Mechanics:**
* **Darkvision:** You gain Darkvision out to 120ft.
* **Eye Rays:** The monocle has **4 charges** and regains 1d4 charges daily at dawn. As a bonus action, you can expend a charge to focus on a target, unleashing a random ray (Roll d4):
    1.  **Fear Ray:** DC 13 Wisdom save or be frightened for 1 minute (repeat save at end of turns).
    2.  **Death Ray:** DC 13 Constitution save or take **3d10 necrotic damage** (half on success).
    3.  **Force Ray:** DC 15 Strength save or be pushed 30ft (half distance on success).
    4.  **Illusion Ray:** DC 13 Intelligence save or believe they are staring at a brick wall in all directions (repeat save at end of turns).

# Dragoneye
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** A chief dragon hunter.

**Description:** Minimalistic rings with a massive crimson gem, said to be dipped in dragon blood.

**Mechanics:**
* **Adaptive Resistance:** Whenever you finish a short or long rest, choose one damage type: **lightning, poison, fire, or cold**. You have resistance to it until your next rest.
* **Legendary Will:** Once per long rest, when you fail a saving throw, you may choose to succeed instead.

# Elixir of Absolution
*Potion, Legendary*

**Origins:** A sinner seeking forgiveness.

**Description:** When shaken, the foam forms faces of people important to the holder before disappearing.

**Mechanics:**
* **Total Erasure:** The potion can only be used once. When drank, the whole universe forgets everything they know about the user (excluding the user themselves).
* **Effects:** Parents forget having a child, crimes are explained away as accidents, and documents verifying existence are written off as false.
* **Note:** You are still a physical creature; enemies in combat will continue to attack you as a "nameless wanderer," though they will not know who you are.

# Flamechaser Bottle
*Wondrous Item, Rare*

**Origins:** A genie warlord.

**Description:** A porcelain bottle covered in engravings of sea creatures.

**Mechanics:**
* **Charges:** The bottle has **3 charges** and regains 1d3 at sunrise (must be touched by sunrays).
* **Extinguish:** Open the bottle and expend 1 charge to immediately extinguish all magical and normal flames within 30 feet (including *Wall of Fire*).
* **Banish Fire:** Any fire elementals (including Fire Snakes/Salamanders) in the area must succeed on a **DC 15 Charisma save** or be banished.
* **Plane of Fire:** The bottle cannot be opened on the Plane of Fire.

# Fool's Chapeau
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Origins:** An infamous gnome prankster.

**Description:** Looks sophisticated on a shelf, but turns into a ridiculous, plumage-covered abomination when worn.

**Mechanics:**
* **Spells:** While wearing the hat, you can cast *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* (DC 15) as an action by expending 1 charge.
* **Charges:** The hat has **3 charges** and regains all charges daily at midnight.
* **Curse:** As soon as it is put on, you look ridiculous. You have **disadvantage on all Persuasion and Intimidation checks**. The hat requires *Remove Curse* to take off.

# Gambler's Club
*Weapon (Any Bludgeoning), Uncommon*

**Origins:** An odd casino that gave items as rewards.

**Description:** Has a massive crystal die at the top that lights up when it hits a creature.

**Mechanics:**
* **Bonus:** You have a **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
* **The Gamble:** Whenever you declare an attack action, roll any die:
    * **Even Result:** All attacks this turn are made with **Advantage**.
    * **Odd Result:** All attacks this turn are made with **Disadvantage**.
* **Commitment:** You cannot cancel your attack after rolling the die.

# Golden Cutlery
*Wondrous Item (Set), Uncommon*

**Origins:** A halfling wizard with an aptitude for cooking.

**Description:** A set including a spoon, fork, and knife, masterfully crafted.

**Mechanics:**
* **The Spoon:** Has 3 charges (regains 1d3 at dawn). Expend 1 charge to cast *Create or Destroy Water* or *Detect Poison and Disease*.
* **The Fork:** Has 3 charges (regains 1d3 at dawn). Expend 1 charge to cast *Goodberry*. You choose the guise of the food (e.g., a roast or bread).
* **The Knife:** Acts as a Dagger (+1 bonus). As a bonus action, can transform into a Shortsword, Longsword, or Greatsword.
* **Set Bonus:** If you possess all three, you can attune to the set. You gain proficiency (and expertise/double proficiency) in **Cook's Utensils** and **Brewer's Supplies**.

# Graverobber's Mittens
*Gauntlets, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*

**Origins:** An influential dungeoneer.

**Description:** Decorated with fine necrotic motifs, often mistaken for work gloves.

**Mechanics:**
* **Undead Resilience:** You have **advantage on Constitution saving throws** caused by undead creatures (e.g., Ghoul's Stench, Wraith's Life Drain).
* **Flight Response:** At the start of your turn, if a hostile undead is within 30ft, you may take the **Dash action as a bonus action**.
